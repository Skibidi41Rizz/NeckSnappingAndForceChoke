# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Force choke: hold an enemy by the neck, 12 HP/s of choking damage, death in about 8 seconds.
- Enemy reaction: convulsions, loss of balance, kicking legs, wheezing for the whole choke.
- Neck snapping on **B** (right) / **Y** (left), using the game's own joint fracture system.
- Release cancels the damage instantly; all changed enemy state is restored on release.
